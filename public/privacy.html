Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Traverse app ("Traverse," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our app.

Information we collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address from your authentication provider.
  • Hotel stay data you enter: Hotel names, locations, check-in / check-out dates, room types, booking sources, and any notes you add about your stays.
  • Friends and social data: Friend connections you create within the app and stay information your friends choose to share with you.
  • Location data: Hotel locations you enter (typed addresses or selected places). We do not collect or track your real-time device location.

How we use your information

  • To provide the core features of the app — saving your stays, displaying them on a map, calculating travel statistics.
  • To enable social features such as friend lists and shared travel feeds.
  • To authenticate you securely on each visit.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore + Firebase Authentication). Communication between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS. Firebase's data security practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database.
  • Mapbox — map tiles and rendering.
  • Google Places API — hotel and address search.
  • Apple Sign In — optional sign-in method.

These providers may receive minimal technical data (IP address, device type) necessary to deliver their service.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers. The only data shared with other users of the app is information you explicitly choose to share via the friends feature.

Your rights

  • Access: You can view all the data the app stores for you directly in the app.
  • Edit: You can edit or delete individual stays at any time.
  • Delete your account: Open the app menu (your avatar in the top right) → Delete account. This permanently removes all your stays, friends, and account data. This action cannot be undone.

Children

Traverse is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
